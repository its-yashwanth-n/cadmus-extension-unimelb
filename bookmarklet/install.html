Cadmus bookmarklets

Drag a button onto your bookmarks bar (Chrome or Firefox):

Cadmus Exam Tools (settings panel)
Can't drag? Paste the contents of exam-tools.txt as a bookmark's URL.

⚡ Cadmus Exam Tools (one-click)
Can't drag? Paste the contents of exam-tools-quick.txt as a bookmark's URL.

Cadmus Exam Setup (settings panel)
Can't drag? Paste the contents of exam-setup.txt as a bookmark's URL.

⚡ Cadmus Exam Setup (one-click)
Can't drag? Paste the contents of exam-setup-quick.txt as a bookmark's URL.

Settings panel: on the Cadmus assessment settings page, click it to open a panel top-right where you can tick or untick each action and click Apply. Your choices are saved. Click the bookmark again to close the panel.

⚡ One-click: applies the choices you last saved in that version's settings panel (or the defaults if you never opened it) straight away, and shows the results bottom-right. Each click runs once.
